# From Zero to ServiceNow Certified: My Certification Journey

It started as a challenge I gave myself during my second year of B.Tech — *"Can I get certified in an enterprise platform while still in college?"*

Spoiler: Yes. And here's how.

## Why ServiceNow?

ServiceNow powers IT workflows for Fortune 500 companies worldwide. Most students don't touch it until they're placed in a big company. I figured — why wait?

After some research, I set a goal: **earn the CSA (Certified System Administrator) and CAD (Certified Application Developer)** before graduating.

## The Prep Process

I spent 3 months studying on the **ServiceNow developer portal** (developer.servicenow.com) — it's free and gives you a personal developer instance to practice on. I built flows, tested business rules, and got really comfortable with the Now Platform UI.

Some things that helped the most:

- **Hands-on practice** over reading docs — build something, break it, fix it
- **Now Learning** pathways — structured and well-paced
- **Mock exams** — there are community-shared quizzes that really expose your weak spots

## The Exam Day

The CSA came first. 60 questions, 90 minutes, mostly scenario-based. I cleared it with a good margin.

The CAD was harder. It tested things like **Client Scripts, Business Rules, UI Policies, Flow Designer** — the stuff you only *really* understand if you've built something with it.

## What It Opened Up

Beyond the certificate itself, the process made me sharper at thinking in **workflows** — how data flows through an enterprise system, how automation replaces manual processes.

That mindset carried directly into my internship at **Liger Games**, where I approached React component design with the same structured, modular thinking.
